Chapter 1 — Bangalore

The Morning the Magic Car Appeared

One sunny morning in Bangalore, Kirav woke up early — as he always did. He brushed his teeth, folded his blanket neatly, and walked outside to find the most incredible thing sitting in the driveway.

A shiny red racing car, with golden wheels that sparkled like sunshine. On the dashboard, a little note read: "For the bravest, most responsible driver in all of Bangalore. — The Road Magic."

Kirav didn't throw the note on the ground. He folded it carefully and placed it in his pocket. Then he climbed in, buckled up, and smiled. Today was going to be the greatest adventure of his life.

🏙️
Chapter 2 — Delhi

Nana's City of Colours

Kirav pressed the big golden button on the dashboard and whoooosh — the car zoomed north, past green fields and big rivers, all the way to Delhi where his Nana lived!

Delhi was huge and loud and smelled of hot parathas. Nana squeezed him tight and said, "My Kirav! You came all by yourself?" Kirav grinned. "I drove carefully, Nana. I stopped at every red light."

In the busy Delhi market, Kirav spotted a chocolate wrapper on the road. Without even thinking, he picked it up and dropped it in the nearest dustbin. A shopkeeper saw him and clapped. "What a fine young man!" he said.

🌊
Chapter 3 — Odisha

Thakurda's Land by the Sea

Next, Kirav pointed his magic car east and drove all the way to Odisha, where his Thakurda (dad's father) lived near rolling hills and a sparkling river.

Thakurda was sitting under a mango tree when the red car pulled up. He laughed a big belly laugh and said, "Ha! My grandson, the driver!" He showed Kirav the famous Jagannath Temple and told him stories about chariots as big as houses — even bigger than Kirav's 20 toy cars put together!

That night, Kirav helped Thakurda set the dinner table and cleared the plates after eating. "You are the most helpful child," Thakurda said softly, ruffling his hair.

🏁
Chapter 4 — Home

The Greatest Racer of All

As the stars began to fill the sky, Kirav drove his magic car back home to Bangalore. Mummy and Dadda were waiting at the door, arms wide open.

"How was your adventure?" asked Dadda. Kirav thought for a moment. "I saw Nana's Delhi and Thakurda's Odisha. I drove through rivers and mountains. And I put every single piece of rubbish in the bin!"

Mummy laughed and hugged him. "That, my love, is exactly what the greatest racers in the world do."

And so Kirav parked his magic red car, washed his hands, and went inside — the best driver, and the kindest boy, on any road in the whole wide world. 🏁
