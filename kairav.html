🏎️ Kairav's Big Road Trip!

From Bangalore to the Whole Wide World

🏙️ Bangalore 🗺️ Delhi 🌊 Odisha 🏠 Bangalore!

🚗 The Story

🌅 Chapter 1 · Bangalore

The Morning the Magic Car Appeared

🌅

One sunny morning in Bangalore, Kairav woke up early — as he always did. He brushed his teeth, folded his blanket neatly, and walked outside to find the most incredible thing sitting in the driveway.

A shiny red racing car, with golden wheels that sparkled like sunshine. On the dashboard, a little note read:

"For the bravest, most responsible driver in all of Bangalore. — The Road Magic."

Kairav folded the note carefully and placed it in his pocket. Then he climbed in, buckled up, and smiled. Today was going to be the greatest adventure of his life.

🏙️ Chapter 2 · Delhi

Nana's City of Colours

🏙️

Kairav pressed the big golden button on the dashboard and whoooosh — the car zoomed north, past green fields and big rivers, all the way to Delhi where his Nana lived!

Delhi was huge and loud and smelled of hot parathas. Nana squeezed him tight and said, "Kullu! My Kullu! You drove all this way by yourself?" Kairav grinned. "I drove carefully, Nana. I stopped at every red light."

In the busy Delhi market, Kairav spotted a chocolate wrapper on the road. Without even thinking, he picked it up and dropped it in the nearest dustbin. A shopkeeper saw him and clapped.

"What a fine young man!" he said.
🌊 Chapter 3 · Odisha

Thakurda's Land by the Sea

🌊

Next, Kairav pointed his magic car east and drove all the way to Odisha, where his Thakurda (dad's father) lived near rolling hills and a sparkling river.

Thakurda was sitting under a mango tree when the red car pulled up. He laughed a big belly laugh and said, "Ha! My grandson, the driver!" He showed Kairav the famous Jagannath Temple and told him stories about chariots as big as houses — even bigger than Kairav's 20 toy cars put together!

"You are the most helpful child," Thakurda said softly, ruffling his hair.
🏁 Chapter 4 · Home!

The Greatest Racer of All

🏁

As the stars began to fill the sky, Kairav drove his magic car back home to Bangalore. Mummy and Dadda were waiting at the door, arms wide open.

"How was your adventure?" asked Dadda. Kairav thought for a moment. "I saw Nana's Delhi and Thakurda's Odisha. I drove through rivers and mountains. Every road, every market, every stop — not one wrapper, not one tiny scrap dared stay on the ground when I was around. The dustbins? They were waiting for me like old friends!"

Mummy laughed and hugged him tight.

"That, my love, is exactly what the greatest racers in the world do."

And so Kairav parked his magic red car, washed his hands, and went inside — the best driver, and the kindest boy, on any road in the whole wide world. 🏁
